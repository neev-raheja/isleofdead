# Isle of Dead

A top-down 2D zombie survival shooter built with Godot 4.

Survive consecutive waves of zombies on an open island. If your health drops to zero, time your inputs to bounce back to life and stay in the fight. Clear all 10 waves to survive until sunrise.

## Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move
- **Mouse**: Aim
- **Left Click / Space**: Shoot / Revive timing
- **R**: Restart (on game over or victory)
